## PRESENTATION DELIVERY NOTES

### Timing Breakdown (10-15 minutes)
- Slides 1-3 (Problem/Opportunity): 2 minutes
- Slides 4-6 (Solution/Traction): 3 minutes
- Slides 7-11 (Team/GTM/Financials): 4 minutes
- Slides 12-15 (Use of Funds/Ask): 3 minutes
- Q&A: 2-3 minutes

### Design Recommendations
- **Color Palette**: 2-3 brand colors (professional)
- **Typography**: 2-3 fonts max, large readable size
- **Visuals**: Data visualizations, customer quotes, product screenshots
- **Narrative Flow**: Problem → Opportunity → Solution → Proof → Team → Path Forward

### Delivery Tips
- **Opening**: Lead with customer impact, not company history
- **Data**: Use specific numbers, not generalities
- **Passion**: Show genuine belief in solving African supply chain problem
- **Specificity**: Avoid buzzwords; use real metrics
- **Confidence**: Founder has successfully done this before
- **Closing**: Leave them thinking "I want to be part of this"

### Pre-Meeting Checklist
- [ ] Practice deck (5-7 times)
- [ ] Prep for top 3 questions (why now, why you, competitive advantage)
- [ ] Bring supporting materials (customer LOIs, pilot data, advisory bios)
- [ ] Research investor thesis + recent investments
- [ ] Practice stories (customer wins, learning moments, "why now" narrative)
