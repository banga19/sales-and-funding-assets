## COMMON OBJECTIONS & RESPONSES

### Investor Objection: "Why will retailers choose you vs. existing wholesalers?"
**Response**: "Existing wholesalers require 50+ unit minimums; we offer flexible bulk at 15-20% discount. Retailers can't access wholesalers directly; we eliminate intermediaries. First-mover network effects + loyalty incentives create stickiness."

### Sales Objection: "Why should construction companies trust a new supplier?"
**Response**: "We're not replacing their suppliers; we're reducing costs. First order is risk-free (refund guarantee). Pilot with 1-2 low-risk materials first. Save them money in first month = instant credibility."

### Partner Objection: "Why give up margin to Sokogate?"
**Response**: "You're sitting at 60% capacity. We bring consistent volume, paying on 30-day terms, no seasonality. Your current customers aren't buying in bulk; we activate new revenue stream. You scale revenue 20-30% without new infrastructure."

- West African distributors actively seeking new revenue streams
