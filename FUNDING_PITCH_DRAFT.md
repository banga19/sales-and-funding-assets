# Funding Pitch: Sokogate Trade Intelligence Agent

## 1. Executive Summary
Sokogate Agent is a multi-agent AI system designed to revolutionize cross-border B2B trade in Africa. By scraping and analyzing real-time data from sokogate.com, the agent identifies high-value leads, automates marketing outreach, and provides strategic insights to global suppliers and African SMEs. We are seeking funding to scale this system across multiple marketplaces and dominate the trade intelligence sector in the region.

## 2. The Problem
- **Information Asymmetry:** African SMEs struggle to find reliable global suppliers with competitive pricing.
- **Market Entry Barriers:** Global suppliers find it difficult to identify and reach high-intent buyers in fragmented African markets.
- **Lack of Real-time Data:** Trade decisions are often based on outdated information, leading to inefficiencies and lost revenue.

## 3. The Solution: Sokogate Agent
An automated intelligence layer that:
- **Scrapes:** Real-time product, pricing, and supplier data.
- **Analyzes:** Uses advanced lead scoring to identify "hot" trade opportunities.
- **Outreaches:** Automates B2B sales and marketing campaigns.
- **Reports:** Provides "State of African Trade" insights for strategic decision-making.

## 4. Value Proposition
- **For Suppliers:** Direct access to verified, high-volume buyers and competitive pricing intelligence.
- **For Buyers:** Streamlined sourcing of high-quality products at the best market rates.
- **For Investors:** A scalable, data-driven SaaS platform with multiple revenue streams (subscriptions, commissions, consulting).

## 5. Traction & Market Insights (Initial Findings)
Our pilot audit of sokogate.com has already identified:
- **12 Major B2B Categories:** Including high-growth sectors like Electronics, Machinery, and Agriculture.
- **Rich Data Sets:** Access to tiered pricing, transaction history, and supplier ratings.
- **Demand Signals:** Ability to track "Transaction Volume" and "Buyer Count" to score lead quality.

## 6. Business Model
- **SaaS Subscription:** Monthly access to the Lead Generation Dashboard and Premium Reports.
- **Success Fees:** Commission on successful trade matches facilitated by the agent.
- **Bespoke Consulting:** Custom market research for large-scale importers/exporters.

## 7. Use of Funds
- **System Scaling:** Expanding the agent to cover additional marketplaces (Alibaba, TradeKey, etc.).
- **AI Enhancement:** Refining the lead scoring and predictive demand algorithms.
- **Marketing & Sales:** Launching automated outreach at scale to onboard the first 1,000 suppliers.
- **Infrastructure:** Robust hosting and database management via Turso/SQLite.
