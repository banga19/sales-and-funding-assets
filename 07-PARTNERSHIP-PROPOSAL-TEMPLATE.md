### Revenue Model: [CHOOSE ONE]


**Option B: Service Fees (Storage + Fulfillment)**
```
Structure: Fixed monthly warehouse fee + per-shipment fulfillment charge

Pricing:
  - Warehouse storage: USD 1-2 per m³ per month
  - Receiving/unloading: USD 50 per shipment
  - Pick & pack: USD 2-3 per item
  - Delivery coordination: USD 3-5 per order
  - Returns processing: USD 2 per return

Example (Monthly):
  - 500 m³ warehouse: USD 750/month
  - 200 shipments received: USD 10,000
  - 5,000 items picked: USD 12,500
  - 200 orders dispatched: USD 600
  - Total revenue: ~USD 24,000/month
```

**Option C: Exclusive Reseller (Direct Inventory Purchase)**
```
Structure: [PARTNER NAME] buys inventory at wholesale; resells at retail with margin

Pricing:
  - Purchase inventory: USD 20,000/month (at cost)
  - Retail markup: 25-30%
  - Your gross profit: USD 5,000-6,000/month
  - Plus commission on referrals to other retailers
```

**Option D: Hybrid (Revenue Share + Base Fee)**
```
Structure: Combination model balancing risk and upside

Pricing:
  - Base monthly fee: USD 3,000 (covers fixed costs)
  - Commission on sales: 8% (variable upside)
  - Minimum volume commitment: 150 shipments/month
  
Example (Monthly):
  - Base fee: USD 3,000
  - 250 shipments × USD 50 avg order value = USD 12,500
  - Commission 8% of USD 12,500 = USD 1,000
  - Total monthly revenue: USD 4,000
```

**[RECOMMENDED FOR THIS PARTNERSHIP: Option D (Hybrid)]**

**Rationale**: Balances [Partner's] fixed cost recovery with upside participation in our growth. Low risk, high reward as we scale.
