## 💡 KEY INSIGHTS FROM YOUR MATERIALS

### Email Strategy
- **Tier 1 (500M+ spend)**: Focus on cost savings % + guarantees
- **Tier 2 (80-150M spend)**: Focus on simplification + pilot approach
- **Tier 3 (25-120M spend)**: Focus on specialization + flexibility

### Multi-Channel Approach
- Email: 3-5% response rate alone
- Email + LinkedIn: 8-12% response rate
- Email + LinkedIn + Phone: 15-20% response rate
- **Key**: Same person, multiple touchpoints within 3-5 days

### Best Practices from Your Templates
- Subject lines under 60 characters
- Emails under 200 words
- Always include specific available times (not "let me know when")
- Reference something specific about prospect (shows research)
- Keep sales-y language minimal ("save 15-20%" OK, "Act now limited offer!" NOT OK)
- Enable email tracking for all sends
- Follow up within 5-7 days if no response

### Dashboard Usage
- Update after each action (email sent → status "Emailed", call completed → status "Call Completed")
- Use notes field for quick reference (constraints, next steps)
- Export weekly for reporting
- Filter by tier to prioritize outreach
