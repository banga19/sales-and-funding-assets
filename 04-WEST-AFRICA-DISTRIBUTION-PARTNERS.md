## PARTNERSHIP NEGOTIATION FRAMEWORKS

### For Large Distributors (Tier 1)

**Initial Approach**:
> "We're building the supply chain platform for African retailers. Your logistics network is perfect to serve this growing market. We handle customer acquisition; you handle fulfillment. Let's pilot with 100 retailers in [City]."

**Key Terms to Negotiate**:
- **Volume Commitment**: 200-500 shipments/month (min)
- **Pricing**: Storage (USD 1-2/m³), Handling (3-5%), Delivery (USD 2-5 per box)
- **Territory**: Exclusive or co-exclusive
- **Performance Metrics**: On-time delivery 95%+, damage rate <2%
- **Term**: 12-24 months with renewal option
- **Ramp Timeline**: Ramp from 50 shipments/mo → 500 shipments/mo over 12 months

### For Retail Networks (Tier 2)

**Initial Approach**:
> "We want to co-market to your retailer customers. We bring new suppliers and better pricing. You bring customer access. 70/30 revenue split favoring you for this market."

**Key Terms**:
- **Exclusivity**: Co-exclusive (allow 1-2 other suppliers, not competitive)
- **Revenue Split**: Your members get 10-15% discount; you get USD 50 per member per order
- **Marketing**: Joint email/SMS campaigns to member base
- **Support**: You handle member relationship, we handle supply
- **Term**: 24 months, yearly renewal
- **Ramp**: 50 retailers → 1,000 retailers by month 12

### For 3PL/Logistics (Tier 3)

**Initial Approach**:
> "You have infrastructure sitting at 60% capacity. We bring consistent volume, paid on 30-day terms, no seasonality. Let's start with a 12-month trial."

**Key Terms**:
- **Volume**: Minimum 200 shipments/month (USD 10K+ monthly revenue)
- **Pricing**: Fixed storage rate + variable fulfillment fees (negotiate based on volume)
- **Exclusivity**: Non-exclusive but preferred pricing
- **Performance**: 98% on-time, <1% damage rate
- **Payment**: 30-60 day terms (negotiable based on volume scale)
- **Growth**: Monthly growth target with scaling discounts (5% discount at 500 ships/mo)

### For Supplier Representatives (Tier 4)

**Initial Approach**:
> "We're creating an export channel for your members. We take on the buyer risk, compliance, and logistics. You get monthly revenue per supplier."

**Key Terms**:
- **Listing Fee**: USD 100-300 per SKU per month (waived if 50+ orders/month)
- **Commission**: 2-5% on sales (negotiable by volume)
- **Quality Standards**: Your quality assurance remains; we handle complaints
- **Term**: 6-12 months with monthly targets
- **Growth Incentive**: Commission discount if 1,000+ units/month

## DEAL STRUCTURES & REVENUE MODELS

### Model 1: Revenue Share on Sales
```
Structure: Partner receives % of retail sales processed through them
Example: 50 retailers × USD 500/month avg order × 15% margin = USD 3,750/month per partner
Pros: Aligned incentives, scalable
Cons: Partner dependent on our sales growth
Best For: Retail networks, logistics with capacity
```

### Model 2: Service Fees (Storage + Fulfillment)
```
Structure: Fixed monthly warehouse + per-shipment fulfillment charges
Example: 500 warehouses = USD 500/mo storage + USD 5 per order fulfillment
Pros: Predictable partner revenue, not sales-dependent
Cons: Higher working capital requirements
Best For: 3PL/warehouse operators
```

### Model 3: Referral/Finder Fees
```
Structure: Per-customer or per-transaction referral commission
Example: USD 25 per retailer referred, USD 2 per order facilitated
Pros: Low friction, pay-for-performance
Cons: Lower partner engagement
Best For: Associations, telecom partnerships
```

### Model 4: Exclusive Reseller/Distributor
```
Structure: Partner buys inventory at wholesale, marks up 25-40%
Example: USD 10K monthly inventory purchase, sells at 30% markup
Pros: Partner fully committed, entrepreneurial incentive
Cons: Partner carries inventory risk
Best For: Large established distributors, committed partners
```

### Model 5: Hybrid (Revenue Share + Base Fee)
```
Structure: Combination of fixed monthly fee + variable commission
Example: USD 2K/month + 8% of sales revenue
Pros: Risk sharing, sustainable for both parties
Cons: More complex accounting
Best For: Strategic key partners
```

## RISK MITIGATION

### Key Risks & Contingencies

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| **Partner Capacity Issues** | Diversify across 3-5 partners per country; build backup arrangements |
| **Quality Standards** | SLA agreements with penalties; audit partner operations quarterly |
| **Regulatory Changes** | Stay current on customs/trade regulations; use compliance advisors |
| **Partner Conflicts** | Clear territorial/exclusivity agreements; tiered commissions reduce competition |
| **Currency Risk** | Lock pricing in USD where possible; use payment platforms with hedging |
| **Customer Disputes** | Clear dispute resolution process; escrow/arbitration in contracts |
