## INVESTMENT PROCESS & TIMELINE ESTIMATES

### Standard Series A Process (by Investor Type)

**Fast Track (6-8 weeks)**:
- Catalyst Fund
- Kenyan Angel Networks
- Safaricom Ventures

**Standard Track (10-14 weeks)**:
- Verod, Bamboo, Acacia
- Regional private equity
- Pan-Africa impact funds

**Institutional Track (16-24 weeks)**:
- IFC, AfDB, KfW
- Development finance institutions
- Larger regional players

### Key Milestones

1. **Weeks 1-2**: Email + warm introductions
2. **Weeks 2-4**: Initial pitch meeting (15-30 min)
3. **Weeks 4-8**: Deep dive & due diligence
4. **Weeks 8-12**: Term sheet negotiation
5. **Weeks 12+**: Legal & closing

---

## OUTREACH STRATEGY

### Phase 1: Warm Introductions (Week 1)
- Leverage existing networks for warm intros to Tier 1 funds
- Goal: Get 3-5 meetings scheduled
- Email template: "Introduction to founder Ultimotradingltd"

### Phase 2: Pitch Meetings (Weeks 1-4)
- 20-minute elevator pitch focused on:
  - Market size (USD 50B+ African supply chain TAM)
  - Team credentials (operational + tech background)
  - Traction (customer logos, pilot results)
  - Use of funds (expansion, technology, team)

### Phase 3: Targeted Outreach (Weeks 2-6)
- Simultaneous outreach to 8-10 tier 1 & 2 funds
- Personalized emails highlighting fund's thesis alignment
- Request: 30-minute exploratory call

### Phase 4: Dataroom + Due Diligence (Weeks 6-12)
- Prepare comprehensive due diligence pack:
  - Pitch deck (12-15 slides)
  - Financial projections (3-year)
  - Cap table + equity structure
  - Team bios & advisors
  - Customer contracts/LOIs
  - Competitive analysis
  - Use of funds breakdown

### Phase 5: Term Sheet to Closing (Weeks 12+)
- Negotiate term sheet (SPA, governance, rights)
- Conduct investor onboarding
- Close funding

## POSITIONING FOR MAXIMUM TRACTION

### Key Narrative Points
1. **Market Problem**: "40%+ of African retailers pay 30-40% premiums due to fragmented supply chains"
2. **Solution**: "Regional B2B logistics platform creating formal supply chains"
3. **Traction**: "[Customer names] already saving 15-20% on bulk materials"
4. **Opportunity**: "USD 2B addressable market; 5,000+ potential customers in Phase 1"
5. **Team**: "[Founder names] - 15+ years logistics/trade experience"
6. **Impact**: "Enable 50K+ informal retailers to formalize; 10K+ jobs created"

### Why Investors Should Back You
✅ Proven founder with operational credibility
✅ Large addressable market with clear willingness to pay
✅ Early traction with marquee customers
✅ Regional expansion play (Kenya → East Africa → West Africa)
✅ Defensible logistics moat
✅ High-margin B2B SaaS economics
✅ Impact thesis (SME enablement + formal employment)

## DECISION MAKERS AT EACH FUND

When reaching out, identify:
- **Managing Partner** (final decision, relationship builder)
- **Investment Director** (process owner, technical due diligence)
- **Associate** (first screen, process coordinator)

Warm introduction to MD or ID is highest probability path.

## NOTES

- Series A tickets in East Africa range USD 300K - $2M typically
- Longer timelines for development finance (16-24 weeks) but patient capital
- Angel networks + early-stage funds move faster (6-10 weeks)
- Diversify across 2-3 tracks simultaneously to reduce single-point-of-failure risk
