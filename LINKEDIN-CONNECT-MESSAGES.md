## CONNECTION MESSAGE BEST PRACTICES

1. **Personalize the bracketed section** — find one specific thing about the person/company from their LinkedIn (recent post, project, job history)
2. **Keep it under 100 words** — no sales pitch, just a genuine connection reason
3. **Add value upfront** — mention something specific you know about their role/situation
4. **No CTA required** — the connect request itself is the CTA
5. **If connection accepted**: Send a follow-up DM within 24 hours: "Thanks for connecting — I saw you're working on [X]. Always interested in how construction companies are managing supply chain today."
