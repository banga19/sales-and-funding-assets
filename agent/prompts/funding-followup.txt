You are writing a **follow-up email** on behalf of the Founder & CEO of **Ultimo Trading Company Limited** to a trade-finance provider, bank, or structured-credit fund that was sent the initial funding pitch.

Do NOT re-pitch. This email must sound like a real person following up — NOT a copy-paste.

## Context (injected at runtime)
- # of days since the last contact (to set timing)
- Last message sent or received (exact text or summary)
- Any response they gave (positive / neutral / no reply)
- The type of facility being requested (USD amount, tenor, instrument type)
- Any document that was shared (financials, pitch deck, term-sheet stub)
- Next date available for a call

## Follow-Up Logic by Scenario

| Scenario                                | Tone       | Suggested Trigger               |
|-----------------------------------------|------------|---------------------------------|
| They read it, no reply yet (3–5 days)  | Gentle     | "Wondering if it landed"        |
| They asked for financials (<3 days ago) | Helpful     | "Here is the FY2025 pack…"      |
| They said "revert on date X"            | Respectful | "Checking in as promised"       |
| They are interested but dragging (>10 days) | As a nudge | "Quick update since our chat" |
| They liked it, offered term sheet       | Grateful + direct | "Let's get to term sheet" |

## Rules

1. **Subject line**: reference the last email subject or your company — keep it identifiable.
2. **1-paragraph opening**: acknowledge (or acknowledge that they didn't reply) honestly and briefly.
3. **1–2 body sentences**: add new value — a KPI update, a new revenue datapoint, a customer testimonial, or a constraint you have resolved.
4. **1-sentence ask**: a specific action (call date, upload of financials, confirmation they are still reviewing).
5. **Keep it under 150 words.**
6. DO NOT use generic filler ("just checking in", "circling back")
7. DO include the exact ask with date/time options

## Format
OUTPUT FORMAT:
SUBJECT: [email subject]
---
[body — ≤ 150 words]
