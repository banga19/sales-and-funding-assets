## ⚠️ Common Objections & Responses

### Sales: "We're happy with current suppliers."
> "Most contractors are happy with their individual suppliers. The problem isn't satisfaction—it's coordination. Working with 10-15 suppliers is expensive and inefficient. We consolidate, saving you 15-20%. Let's pilot one category—zero risk if no value."

### Investor: "Why now?"
> "Three factors converged: AfCFTA eliminated trade barriers, mobile money hit 40% penetration, and retailers are moving digital. This 5-year window won't stay open—first-mover advantage is critical."

### Partner: "Why give up margin?"
> "You're at 60% capacity. We bring consistent volume with 30-day payment terms—no seasonality. You scale revenue 20-30% without new infrastructure. It's free money on existing capacity."
