## Slide 1 — Title
- Sokogate
- Connecting African Construction Buyers with Global Suppliers
- Tagline: "Bulk procurement, simplified."

## Slide 2 — The Problem
- Construction in Africa faces 25–40% material cost inflation from fragmented supply chains
- Buyers rely on multiple intermediaries → higher costs, no quality assurance, delayed deliveries
- Suppliers struggle to reach creditworthy buyers across borders

## Slide 3 — The Solution
- B2B marketplace connecting vetted suppliers with verified buyers
- Bulk procurement with transparent pricing, logistics tracking, and quality assurance
- One platform for sourcing steel, cement, roofing, electrical, and MEP materials

## Slide 4 — Market Opportunity
- East Africa construction market: $35B+ (2026)
- West Africa construction market: $45B+ (2026)
- Target: 12+ African markets within 3 years
- Initial focus: Kenya (buyers) ↔ West Africa (suppliers)

## Slide 5 — Business Model
- Commission: 12–15% per transaction
- Tiered subscription for buyers (Free / Pro / Enterprise)
- Supplier listing fees + premium placement
- Estimated unit economics: 35% gross margin at scale
