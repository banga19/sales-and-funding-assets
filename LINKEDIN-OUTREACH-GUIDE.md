## LINKEDIN MESSAGING STRATEGY

### Why LinkedIn?
- **Multi-channel approach** (email + LinkedIn = higher response rate)
- **Credibility**: Profile shows experience + customer reviews
- **Warm introduction**: LinkedIn connection feels more personal than cold email
- **Easy follow-up**: Can message after they accept connection request

### Expected Response Rates
- Email alone: 3-5% response rate
- Email + LinkedIn connection: 8-12% response rate
- Email + LinkedIn + follow-up call: 15-20% response rate

## STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION (Tuesday)

### Phase 1: Research (1 hour)
1. Search LinkedIn: "[Company Name]" 
2. Identify the Procurement/Operations decision maker
3. Check their profile for:
   - Job title
   - Years at company
   - Recent activity/posts
   - Mutual connections (if any)
4. Note: Any recent posts you can reference (shows you've done homework)

**Example**: Britam Group Construction → Search results → Filter by "Procurement Director" → Open profile → Review recent posts → Note if they posted about new projects/expansitions

### Phase 2: Customize Message (15 min per prospect)
1. Use one of the templates above
2. Personalize with:
   - Decision maker's actual first name
   - Specific company detail (recent project, industry segment, location)
   - Relevant pain point (if you've researched)
3. Keep under 100 words (LinkedIn prefers shorter messages)
4. No emojis or sales-y language

### Phase 3: Send Connection Request (30 seconds per prospect)
1. Open prospect's LinkedIn profile
2. Click **"Connect"** button
3. A dialog appears: "Add a note?" → YES
4. Paste your customized message (max 300 characters, so keep it SHORT)
5. Click **"Send"**

**Note**: LinkedIn allows 1-2 sentence note on connection request. For longer message, wait for them to accept → then send full message

### Alternative If LinkedIn Only Allows Short Notes
1. Send connection request with short note: "Hi [Name], would love to connect on supply chain opportunities. [Your name]"
2. Wait 1-2 days for them to accept
3. After acceptance → Send longer message via LinkedIn DM

## POST-CONNECTION STRATEGY

### After They Accept Connection (1-3 Days Later)
1. **Wait 24 hours** for acceptance
2. Open their profile → click **"Message"**
3. Send your full outreach message:
   - Expand on value proposition
   - Include link to Calendly for 15-min call
   - Keep professional but warm tone

**Example Follow-Up DM**:
```
Hi David,

Great connecting! Quick thought—given Britam's scale and multi-project 
operations, there's likely 15-20% savings opportunity in your material 
procurement.

I specialize in exactly this. Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call 
this week to explore? I can share preliminary analysis beforehand.

Available: Tue 2pm, Wed 10am, Thu 9am

Let me know,
[Your Name]
```

### If They Don't Accept (After 7 Days)
1. **Try alternative**: Connect with someone else from same company
2. **Email backup**: Send your outreach email to their work email (usually firstname.lastname@companyname.co.ke)
3. **LinkedIn search again**: Try finding different decision maker (e.g., if Procurement Director didn't respond, try VP Operations)

## MULTI-CHANNEL PLAYBOOK (Email + LinkedIn + Phone)

**Day 1 (Tuesday)**: Send email + LinkedIn connection
- Prospect sees both channels
- Higher visibility

**Day 3 (Thursday)**: If no response, send LinkedIn DM
- They may have missed email
- LinkedIn DM is harder to miss

**Day 5-7 (Friday-Monday)**: If still no response, make phone call
- "Hi [Name], I sent you an email earlier this week about Sokogate. Did you get a chance to see it? Wanted to see if we could grab 15 minutes..."
- Breaks through noise

**Multi-Channel Result**: 15-20% response rate vs. 3-5% email alone

## LinkedIn BEST PRACTICES

✅ DO:
- Customize every connection request message
- Reference something specific about their company/role
- Keep messages short (under 100 words)
- Send connection + email on same day (multi-channel)
- Wait 1-3 days before sending DM (gives them time to accept)
- Update tracking sheet after each action

❌ DON'T:
- Use generic connection request ("I'd like to add you to my network")
- Copy-paste same message to everyone
- Mention Sokogate in first message (too sales-y)
- Send connection + DM same time (looks spammy)
- Connect then immediately ask for call (feels pushy)
- Forget to track (you'll lose continuity)

## RESPONSE SCENARIOS

### Scenario 1: They Accept + Don't Respond to DM
**Action**: Wait 3 days → Send follow-up DM: "Hi [Name], checking in—are you available for a quick call this week?"

### Scenario 2: They Accept + Positive Response
**Action**: Send your Calendly link → Schedule call immediately → Update dashboard status to "Call Scheduled"

### Scenario 3: They Decline/Don't Accept
**Action**: 
- If decision maker, wait 7 days → Try connecting with alternate contact at company
- Make note in tracking sheet: "Try [Alternative Name]" 
- Still send email to original contact (they may forward)

### Scenario 4: They Respond But Say "Not Interested"
**Action**: 
- Be gracious: "No problem, totally understand. Let's stay connected—might be relevant later."
- Update dashboard: Status = "Not Interested"
- Move on to next prospect

## ANTI-SPAM TIPS

LinkedIn limits connection requests: ~100/week per account
- Don't send 100 requests in one day (flagged as spam)
- Spread over 3-5 days throughout week
- Mix in accepting others' requests
- Only request people relevant to your business (not random profiles)

**Safe pace**: 10-15 connection requests per day maximum
