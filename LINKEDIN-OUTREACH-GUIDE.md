## Connection Request Templates

### Template A — Investor (Private Equity / Impact VC)

> Hi [Name], I came across [Fund Name]'s work in African markets — impressive portfolio. I'm building Sokogate, a B2B marketplace connecting construction buyers across East & West Africa with vetted suppliers. We're seeing strong traction with our bulk procurement model. Would be great to connect and share notes.

### Template B — Partner (Logistics / Distribution / Trade)

> Hi [Name], I've been following [Company]'s work in West African trade/logistics. Sokogate is building a B2B procurement platform for construction materials across 12+ African markets. We're looking for strategic logistics partners to support our West Africa corridor. Would love to connect.

### Template C — Prospect (Construction / Real Estate Developer)

> Hi [Name], I noticed your work at [Company] in Nairobi's construction sector. Sokogate helps contractors reduce bulk material costs by 15–20% through direct West African supplier access. Would be great to connect and see if there's a fit for your upcoming projects.

### Template D — Industry Contact / Referral Source

> Hi [Name], I'm building Sokogate — a platform that connects African construction buyers with vetted suppliers across the continent. Would love to connect and learn from your experience in the [industry/region] space.

## Execution Workflow

1. **Search** for the target on LinkedIn
2. **Review** their profile — recent posts, shared connections, company updates
3. **Personalize** the connection request template with a specific detail from their profile
4. **Send** connection request (max 300 characters)
5. **Track** in `LINKEDIN-TRACKER.csv` or METRICS-DASHBOARD

> Note: LinkedIn has daily connection request limits (~100/week for new accounts, ~25/week for established). Start with 5/week.

## Messaging After Connection

Once connected, wait 2–3 days, then send a brief message:

> Thanks for connecting! Sokogate helps [target group] with [value prop]. Would you be open to a 10-min chat this week?
