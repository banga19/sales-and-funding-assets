# Funding and Grant Opportunities Summary

This document outlines the identified funding sources and grants that align with the Sokogate Agent's goal of scaling cross-border B2B trade intelligence in Africa.

## 1. Future Africa
- **Type:** Venture Capital
- **Amount Range:** $50k - $250k (Initial check)
- **Fit:** Future Africa is one of the most active early-stage investors in African tech. They focus on sectors that solve infrastructure gaps, and our agent's focus on SME sourcing and trade optimization aligns perfectly with their mission to build "the future of Africa."

## 2. Ventures Platform
- **Type:** Venture Capital
- **Amount Range:** $50k - $1M
- **Fit:** They specialize in "category-leading" companies in Africa. Since the Sokogate agent creates a unique bridge between global supply and local demand through data, it fits their criteria for innovative, tech-driven trade solutions.

## 3. Google for Startups Accelerator: Africa
- **Type:** Accelerator / Equity-Free Support
- **Amount Range:** $50k - $100k (Equivalent in credits and support)
- **Fit:** This program supports startups using AI and data to solve local problems. Our agent is a prime candidate as it leverages automated intelligence to facilitate international trade for African SMEs.

## 4. USAID "Prosper Africa" Initiative
- **Type:** Grant / Strategic Partner
- **Amount Range:** $100k - $5M
- **Fit:** Prosper Africa aims to increase two-way trade between the US and Africa. By identifying high-quality global suppliers (including those in the US) and matching them with African buyers, our agent directly supports the initiative's trade facilitation goals.

## 5. African Development Bank (AfDB) SME Programs
- **Type:** Grant / Development Funding
- **Amount Range:** $50k - $500k
- **Fit:** The AfDB provides significant support for digital inclusion and SME growth. Our agent empowers African SMEs by providing them with competitive sourcing data, which is a key priority for AfDB's economic development strategy.
