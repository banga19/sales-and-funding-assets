## 💡 PRO TIPS FOR SUCCESS

### Sales:
1. **Personalization matters**: 70% response rate with personalized emails vs. 10% with generic
2. **First win gets everything**: Close one pilot fast → use as social proof for others
3. **Weekly follow-ups**: Track responses, follow up consistently
4. **CRM discipline**: Log every interaction; don't lose leads

### Fundraising:
1. **Warm intros first**: 50% meeting rate vs. 5% cold email rate
2. **Multiple tracks**: Pursue fast-track + institutional simultaneously
3. **Traction is everything**: Investor interest jumps 10x with paying customers
4. **Practice**: Practice pitch 100+ times; perfection matters

### Partnerships:
1. **Non-exclusive is easier**: Partners less threatened if they're not exclusive
2. **Revenue share aligns incentives**: Better than flat fees
3. **Pilot first**: Reduce risk; prove model before full commitment
4. **Quick wins**: Close 1 partnership → use as leverage for others

### General:
1. **Velocity = momentum**: Move fast; close deals when interest is high
2. **Rejection is data**: Each "no" teaches you something; iterate
3. **Update everything weekly**: CRM, metrics, forecasts
4. **Celebrate wins**: Close each pilot/investor meeting; keep energy high
