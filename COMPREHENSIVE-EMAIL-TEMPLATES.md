## PERSONALIZATION CHECKLIST

**Before sending ANY email, verify**:
- [ ] Contact's actual name (LinkedIn or company website)
- [ ] Company name spelled correctly
- [ ] One reference to company news/project/growth (if available)
- [ ] Relevant pain point for their industry/tier
- [ ] Specific KES amounts (if available in prospect list)
- [ ] Available times are realistic (don't offer Sokogate hours your team can't keep)
- [ ] Email address is verified (company website or LinkedIn)
- [ ] No typos
- [ ] Email tracking enabled

---

## SENDING BEST PRACTICES

✅ DO:
- Send Tue-Thu, 9-11am or 2-4pm (business hours)
- Personalize with 1-2 sentences specific to company
- Keep subject line under 60 characters
- Keep email under 200 words
- Always include specific available times (not vague: "let me know your availability")
- Include tracking + save to follow-up folder
- Follow up within 5-7 days if no response

❌ DON'T:
- Send on Fridays (low response rates)
- Send before 8am or after 5pm
- Use generic "Hi there" or "To whom it may concern"
- Send attachments in first email
- Use sales-y language ("Limited time offer", "Act now")
- Forget to track responses
- Miss follow-up window
