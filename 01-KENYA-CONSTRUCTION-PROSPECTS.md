## OUTREACH STRATEGY

### Phase 1: Initial Contacts (Week 1-2)
- Email procurement directors at Tier 1 & 2 companies
- Subject: "Reduce Material Costs by 15-20% | Bulk Sourcing Partnership"
- Attach: 1-page cost-saving analysis

### Phase 2: Stakeholder Meetings (Week 3-4)
- Schedule 15-minute discovery calls with 8-10 hot prospects
- Discuss current supplier challenges and procurement volumes
- Qualify for pilot program

### Phase 3: Pilot Program (Month 2)
- Launch pilot with 2-3 enterprises
- Deliver measurable savings on first order
- Capture testimonials and case studies

### Phase 4: Expansion (Month 3+)
- Case study marketing to warm tier contacts
- Referral program for successful pilots
- Enterprise account management structure
